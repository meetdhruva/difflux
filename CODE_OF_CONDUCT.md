# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want difflux to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer,
[@meetdhruva](https://github.com/meetdhruva), using the contact options on
their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove content, or warn, suspend or ban contributors whose behavior violates
this code, proportionate to the harm caused.
